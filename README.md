# Kanduck for Claude

Plan projects and triage your day on your [Kanduck](https://kanduck.com) boards without leaving Claude.

Kanduck is a simple kanban board that works with AI assistants. This plugin connects Claude to it and adds two skills.

## What you get

- **Connector:** the Kanduck remote server at `https://kanduck.com/mcp`. Claude can list and read your boards, create boards and lists, add, edit, copy and move cards, set labels, search, and show recent activity. Deleting a list or card always asks first.
- **Skill `plan-a-project`:** turns a goal or a messy list into a board with lists, cards, checklists and due dates.
- **Skill `daily-triage`:** shows what is due, overdue or stuck across your boards and moves cards to match.

## Sign in

The first time Claude uses Kanduck, a Kanduck page opens and asks you to approve the connection. Choose "Read and edit my boards" or "Read only", and optionally limit it to one board. You can disconnect any time in Kanduck under Invite your AI. Nothing to copy or paste.

You need a Kanduck account. Kanduck is free for up to 10 people. Start at [kanduck.com/app](https://kanduck.com/app).

## Try it

- "Plan our November launch on a Kanduck board."
- "What should I work on today?"
- "Add these five tasks to my Home board."

## Privacy

Claude reads and changes only the boards you approve. See the [privacy policy](https://kanduck.com/privacy). Questions: support@kanduck.com.
